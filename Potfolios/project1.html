Particular Symptoms to Watch For

Parent Quick-Check: If your child exhibits any of the following indicators, please book an evaluation promptly.

Persistent High Fever

Fever exceeding 38.5°C that does not subside with standard treatment, especially in infants under 3 months.

Respiratory Distress

Rapid breathing, wheezing, flaring nostrils, or rib retractions when breathing.

Severe Lethargy

Unusual difficulty waking up, reduced responsiveness, or persistent lack of interest in surroundings.
