Childhood Illnesses & Warning Signs

Parent synopsis: If your child displays any of these symptoms, please reach out or book a consultation.

High Priority

🌡️ High Fever & Lethargy

Persistent high fever lasting more than 48 hours, accompanied by fatigue or unresponsiveness.

Respiratory

🫁 Wheezing & Persistent Cough

Shortness of breath, rapid chest expansion, or tight coughing interrupting sleep.

Gastrointestinal

🤢 Severe Dehydration / Vomiting

Repeated fluid loss, lack of wet diapers, or refusal to take liquids.

Dermatology

🎨 Sudden Unexplained Rashes

Rashes accompanied by fever or red spots that do not pale when pressed.
