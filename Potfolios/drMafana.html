About The Practitioner

A compassionate partner in your child’s health journey.

Dr. T. Mafana understands that children need more than just clinical treatment—they need patience, a gentle touch, and an environment where they feel safe.

She works closely with parents, offering expert medical guidance alongside practical support, ensuring that both child and caregiver feel empowered every step of the way.
